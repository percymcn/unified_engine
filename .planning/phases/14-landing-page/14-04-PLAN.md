# Phase 14 Plan 04: Pricing Section & Comparison

## Objective

Create an integrated pricing section on the landing page with competitor comparison.

Purpose: Help visitors understand pricing and see Tradeflow's advantages over alternatives.
Output: Pricing cards and comparison table sections.

## Context

### Existing Pricing Page
- `/pricing` already exists with PricingCard component
- Plans: Free ($0), Pro ($29/mo)
- Reuse plan data but create landing-specific layout

### Competitor Comparison
Position against:
- Manual trading (slow, error-prone)
- Other webhook services (Alertzy, etc.)
- Custom scripts (maintenance burden)
